# AI Use Disclosure

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

Applyr includes optional AI-assisted features. This disclosure explains what they do and what to expect when you use them. They are tools for your own review and do not replace your judgment or professional advice.

## Features that may use AI

Depending on the version of Applyr, AI may be used to:

- extract company, role, description, and language information from job-page content;
- extract text from an uploaded resume or PDF; and
- generate or refine resume content based on your instructions and a selected job.

AI features are optional. You can use the application tracker without using AI features.

## What is sent to an AI provider

When you activate an AI feature, you direct Applyr to send the relevant content needed to produce the requested result. This can include resume text, job-description text, your instructions, and technical metadata needed to handle the request.

Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card data, or unrelated confidential information in prompts.

## Accuracy and responsibility

AI output can contain errors, invented details, biased language, or wording that does not accurately describe your experience. You must review every result before saving, sharing, or submitting it, and you remain solely responsible for any content you use. Applyr does not make hiring decisions, does not submit applications for you, and does not guarantee interviews, employment, or compatibility with any applicant-tracking system.

## Training and retention

Applyr currently uses OpenRouter for AI-assisted requests. We review provider terms and settings for retention, confidentiality, processing location, and model-training practices as the Service evolves.

Applyr’s operational logs may include prompt text, generated output, and usage metadata for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and cost accounting. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for more information.

## Questions

Questions about AI processing can be sent to **applyr.app@gmail.com**.
