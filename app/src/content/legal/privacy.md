# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Walid Idrissi**, based in Morocco and trading as Applyr (“Applyr”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects and uses personal data through the Applyr website, application, browser extension, and related services.

## 1. Data we process

Depending on how you use Applyr, we may process:

- account details such as your name, email address, password credentials, email-verification status, and authentication-provider identifier;
- job-application records such as companies, roles, statuses, dates, notes, tasks, reminders, tags, and notifications;
- resumes, cover letters, job postings, PDFs, extracted text, and other documents you upload or create;
- prompts, instructions, and generated results used with AI-assisted features;
- technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, logs, security events, and approximate usage information; and
- messages you send to us, including support and privacy requests.

Please avoid uploading sensitive personal data unless it is necessary for your job-search workspace. Resumes can sometimes contain information about health, disability, identity, nationality, or other legally protected characteristics. We do not need those details to provide ordinary application-tracking features.

You are responsible for deciding what information to upload and for ensuring that you have the right to provide any personal data that relates to another person.

## 2. How we use data

We use personal data to:

- create and secure your account;
- store, organize, display, export, and delete your applications and documents;
- provide reminders, notifications, resume tools, and job-import features;
- process AI requests that you initiate;
- provide support and communicate about the Service;
- prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, and enforce our Terms; and
- understand reliability and improve the Service, using aggregated or minimized information where practical.

We do not sell resume content or application records. We do not use private user content to train a general-purpose AI model unless we clearly disclose that change and obtain any consent or other authorization required by applicable law.

## 3. Legal bases and local requirements

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, the basis may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required.

Applyr handles personal data from Morocco and may also serve users elsewhere. We will meet applicable registration, declaration, authorization, and cross-border-transfer requirements, including any requirements of the Moroccan CNDP. Users in jurisdictions with additional privacy rights may exercise those rights as described below.

## 4. Service providers and international transfers

We may share the minimum data needed with providers that help us operate the Service, including:

- hosting, database, and private file-storage providers;
- Brevo, which delivers transactional email;
- Google, when you choose Google sign-in;
- OpenRouter, when you use an AI-assisted feature; and
- security, monitoring, or analytics providers, if enabled.

Some providers may process data outside Morocco or your country. We will use the safeguards and approvals required by applicable law.

## 5. AI processing

When you use resume generation, resume extraction, or job extraction, relevant content may be sent to an AI provider to return the requested result. We aim to minimize the content sent and to use providers whose terms address confidentiality, retention, and model training. See the [AI Use Disclosure](/legal/ai-use).

AI request logs may include prompt text, generated output, and usage metadata. We may retain these logs for a limited period for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and cost accounting. Please do not include passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information, or unrelated confidential information in AI prompts.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and User Content while your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may delete applications, documents, resumes, or your account through available controls or by contacting us.

After deletion, copies may remain temporarily in encrypted backups, security logs, or records we must retain by law.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal data, including access controls, authentication, rate limiting, secure transport, private file storage, logging, backups, and service monitoring. No internet service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or alteration will never occur.

We will maintain an incident-response process and notify affected people or authorities where required by applicable law.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, email **applyr.app@gmail.com** with the account email and the request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

## 9. Children

Applyr is not directed to children who are not legally permitted to use the Service. Do not use the Service if you cannot legally agree to these terms.

We may update this Policy as the Service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice where required.
